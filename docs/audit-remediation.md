## 2026-09-21 independent review

A separate full-scope review covered all 17 instructions against the Solana/sealevel attack taxonomy, the Pina framework's enforcement semantics (verified against crate source, not assumed), the money paths, the pricing controller, CI, the server signing path, the app's key handling, and the operations layer.

Code-layer result: no Critical or High finding. Every historical loss class in scope was already closed — no client-supplied sysvars (the Wormhole class), BIT bound to the config-stored mint with revoked authorities and no extensions (the Cashio class), canonical-bump enforcement on every PDA creation (the Crema class), vault to section-ATA binding (the OpenBook role-confusion class), `overflow-checks = true` plus checked math throughout (the Cetus class), and no oracle or price-feed dependency (the Mango/Solend class). Two findings had already been fixed on `main` by the time of this review: protocol-fee withdrawal (`WithdrawProtocolFees`) and the sealed/minted vault-funding gate.

Still required before mainnet, unchanged by this review unless noted:

- independent program and operator audit;
- the authority and custody sign-off checklist, completed and recorded;
- durable mint-reconciliation worker (BF-05) and a distributed ingress rate limit (BF-10);
- the final marketplace-authenticity decision in ADR 0002 and the Serverpod policy decision;
- operations evidence: staging, restore, soak, and canary drills.

- Web/devnet beta: code-ready after PR checks, but deployment-ready only after staging, restore, soak, observability, and canary evidence exists.
- Android/devnet beta: code-ready after PR checks, but store-ready only after release-key/internal-track and remaining physical-device evidence.
- iOS beta: embedded-wallet code-ready; signing, TestFlight and physical Keychain/transaction validation remain external gates. macOS remains view-only.
- Mainnet: blocked by the independent audit/ceremony, durable worker, final authenticity decision, Serverpod policy, and completed operations evidence.
